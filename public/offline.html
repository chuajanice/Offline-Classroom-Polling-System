How to Play

Choose a game duration of 30 or 60 seconds.

  • Computer: Type the displayed word and press Enter.
  • Touchscreen: Tap the letter tiles in the correct order. Use Undo or Clear if needed.
  • Earn 1 point for every correctly completed word.
  • Try to complete as many words as possible before time runs out.

Automatic Difficulty Progression

The game automatically increases the difficulty as time passes. You cannot manually select a difficulty level.

Easy0–25% of game time3–4 letters
Medium25–50% of game time4–5 letters
Hard50–75% of game time6–8 letters
Expert75–100% of game time8–12 letters

Longer words appear as you progress. The difficulty is based on the percentage of time elapsed, not the number of words completed.
